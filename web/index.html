RECORDS TO LOOK FOR

Can we isolate phishing from other SMS scams?

The April–September 2025 snapshot lists phishing and scams separately at 18% each. The following snapshot groups them together at 29%. Those percentages describe SMS spam reported via the online form.

A focused records search would seek existing monthly counts of online-form SMS complaints coded as phishing, the total SMS complaints used as their denominator, and the code definitions in force from April 2025 through March 2026. The public snapshots also leave open how many reported domains were referred or suspended.

The completed-request index lists ISED release A-2026-00004 for the 2024–25 CASL performance measurement report. Its June 2026 summary records 43 pages disclosed in full. Requesting that release can establish which measures it covers.

Coverage and definitions

These six-month CRTC snapshots describe reports received before validation. One person or domain can appear in multiple submissions. The published counts cannot establish the number of victims, unique domains, confirmed incidents or takedowns. Missing categories have no published value here. A weekly average does not reveal week-by-week counts.

Data are bundled with the site and reviewed against each linked publication. The CSV preserves units, qualifiers, original category labels and source URLs. The JSON snapshot also includes notes about discrepancies within the source. All selections are processed in your browser. CoolCode’s shared visit counter records a page visit on coolcode.ca.
